How to read this page: each story carries a genuineness badge based on how many independent outlets reported it — Verified (3+ outlets), Corroborated (2 outlets), Single source (1 outlet — treat with caution). Corroboration means multiple outlets reported it; it is not proof the story is true. Always open the source links and judge for yourself.
Methodology & limitations
  • Stories are collected from Google News RSS (Patna/Bihar queries, English + Hindi) and a hand-verified list of web/social finds. No story is invented — every card links to real sources.
  • Headlines saying the same thing are grouped by keyword overlap; outlet counts use distinct outlets, not raw link counts.
  • Only stories from the last 7 days are shown. Auto-collected items need 2+ outlets to appear; hand-verified items always appear, even single-source.
  • Coverage is best-effort: some outlets block automated collection, and social platforms (Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube) are only represented via manually collected links. X/Twitter yielded no usable public links at last check.
  • To regenerate: python3 generate.py (standard library only).
